# CV

The content model behind Conor's personal CV site: the career history, side projects and technologies it presents.

## Language

### Career

**Employment**:
A single continuous stint at a Company, shown under its current title. A promotion updates the existing Employment; leaving and rejoining starts a new one.
_Avoid_: Experience, role, position, job

**Company**:
An organisation Conor has been employed by.
_Avoid_: Employer, organisation

### Projects

**Project**:
Something Conor built outside of work, presented on the CV with a description and the Technologies used. A Project may or may not have an App.
_Avoid_: Side project, portfolio item

**App**:
A shipped product behind a Project, with its own name, logo and links. Each App belongs to exactly one Project, and its source may be private.
_Avoid_: Application, product

### Technologies

**Technology**:
A language, framework, tool or platform that can be referenced from anywhere on the CV.
_Avoid_: Tech, tool, skill

**Toolkit**:
A captioned group of Technologies describing how Conor relates to them, such as daily professional use or personal preference.
_Avoid_: Skill, skill group, stack
